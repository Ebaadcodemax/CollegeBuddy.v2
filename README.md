# 🎓 CollegeBuddy

**CollegeBuddy** is a campus-specific discovery platform designed to help college students—especially freshers—navigate campus life easily.

Think of it as a **single hub for everything on campus**: clubs, events, services, peers, and essential resources.

---

## 🚀 Problem

New college students struggle with:
- Discovering clubs and communities
- Finding campus services and facilities
- Staying updated with events
- Connecting with like-minded peers
- Accessing verified, centralized campus information

Information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and word-of-mouth.

---

## 💡 Solution

**CollegeBuddy** centralizes all campus-related information into one clean, intuitive platform.

### Core Capabilities
- 📍 Campus-specific onboarding
- 🏫 Verified listings for:
  - Clubs & societies
  - Events & workshops
  - Campus services (libraries, labs, canteens, hostels, etc.)
- 👥 Student & peer discovery
- 🔔 Updates and announcements
- 🤖 AI-powered summaries and recommendations *(planned)*

---

## 🧠 Vision

To become the **default digital companion for every college student**, starting from Day 1 on campus.

Long-term goals:
- Smart recommendations based on interests
- AI-powered campus assistants
- Inter-college discovery network
- Verified student communities

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Planned / MVP)

- **Backend:** Node.js, Express
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript (React planned)
- **Auth:** JWT-based authentication
- **AI (Future):** Summarization & recommendation systems
